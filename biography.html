Biography

I am currently pursuing undergraduate studies at Tsinghua University in mathematical sciences and interdisciplinary engineering (Operations Research × Artificial Intelligence).

My coursework and independent study have focused on probability, statistics, optimization, operations research, and reinforcement learning. I am also learning to implement and train language models, with an interest in connecting theoretical ideas to real model behavior.

Research interests

Reinforcement learning, online learning, multi-agent learning, learning under limited feedback, and algorithms for language-model reasoning and post-training.
