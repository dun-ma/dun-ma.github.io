Research

Research interests

My current interests center on sequential decision-making with limited feedback, including online learning, multi-agent learning, reinforcement learning, and applications to modern language models.

  • Learning from limited feedback. Aggregate rewards, trajectory-level feedback, pairwise preferences, and their implications for learning algorithms.
  • Reinforcement learning and exploration. Sample efficiency, regret minimization, concentration, and adaptive decision-making.
  • RL for language models. Reasoning, reward signals, post-training, and long-horizon interaction.

Current study and exploration

I am strengthening my theoretical background in bandits and reinforcement learning while developing practical experience in training small language models. These interests inform my future research direction; they are not a list of completed research results.
