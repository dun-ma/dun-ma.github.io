About me

I am an undergraduate student at Tsinghua University, studying mathematical sciences and interdisciplinary engineering, with a focus on operations research and artificial intelligence.

I am interested in reinforcement learning, online learning, and sequential decision-making. I am currently building a stronger foundation in modern language models, particularly reinforcement learning for reasoning, post-training algorithms, and learning from limited feedback.

Research summary

My broad interests lie at the intersection of learning theory and modern foundation models. I am especially interested in how learning agents can improve from sparse, delayed, or imperfect feedback; how exploration and statistical estimation affect sequential decisions; and how these ideas can inform language-model reasoning and interactive agents.

I enjoy mathematical modeling, algorithm design, and rigorous analysis, and I am developing the experimental skills needed to test these ideas in practical learning systems.

Research areas

  • reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making
  • online learning and bandit problems
  • learning from aggregate, preference, and trajectory-level feedback
  • LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning for post-training
  • agentic learning and exploration
